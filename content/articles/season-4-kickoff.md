Three years in, and we're still here. Jimmy and Matt are thrilled to welcome you to **season four** of the show.

## What's new this season

We've spent the break listening — to you, and to our own back catalogue. A few things we're changing:

- **Shorter, tighter episodes.** You told us the two-hour marathons were a lot. We heard you.
- **A proper community forum.** Every episode now has a discussion thread right here on the site.
- **Articles alongside episodes.** Longer write-ups (like this one) for the stuff that doesn't fit into audio.

## What we learned from three years of podcasting

Honestly? That consistency beats perfection. Some of our favourite episodes were recorded in one take on a Tuesday night, and some of the ones we polished for weeks landed with a thud. Season four is about showing up every week and trusting the conversation.

> The best episode is the one you actually publish.

## How to get involved

1. Subscribe in your podcast app so new episodes land automatically.
2. Jump into the [forum](/forum) and tell us what you want to hear.
3. Send this site to one friend who'd enjoy the show — that's still how most people find us.

New episodes drop soon. See you there.

— Jimmy & Matt
